In January 2025, a worker in Texas was repairing the chain of a packing machine when it was switched on. He lost the tip of his finger.

It was reported to OSHA, like thousands of injuries every year. In most companies the risk behind it, a machine that can start during repairs, sits in a spreadsheet that nobody opens.

Aravalli gives that risk an owner, a score and a review date, and links the next incident straight back to it.

Know which risks matter most

Every risk gets an owner and a score from 1 to 25, likelihood times impact. The heat map shows where the dangerous ones sit, and old scores stay in the history so you can see whether a risk is getting better.
